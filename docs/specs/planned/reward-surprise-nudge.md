## Problem

Catch / Cross / Match are enough for soft beta, but over a formal beta month kids
may always tap the same game. Cheap perceived variety (Surprise-first or
don’t-repeat-last) beats shipping new engines before learning whether habit
sticks.

## Non-goals (sketch)

- New mini-game engines or platformers
- AI skins (`ai-game-variants`)
- Free-play outside reward (`free-play-games`)
- Changing skip-safe unlock rules

## Notes

- Formal-beta slice after soft-beta learnings; promote earlier only if soft-beta
  kids complain about sameness.
- Keep tablet-safe; no Nintendo IP / Mario clones.
