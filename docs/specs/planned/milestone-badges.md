## Problem

Beyond mini-games, parents want an extra carrot for big moments — first *liked*
new food, then milestones like 5 / 10 / 25 new foods tried or liked. A simple
badge / celebration beat makes progress visible without turning the app into a
points grind.

## Non-goals (sketch)

- Full gamification / leaderboards / streaks across households
- Free-play games (`free-play-games`)
- Fancy confetti FX as a hard dependency (`reward-celebrate-fx` can follow)

## Notes

- Define “new food” precisely at `/spec` (truly_new vs first like, etc.).
- Keep celebrations calm and brand-aligned.
