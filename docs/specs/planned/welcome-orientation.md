## Problem

New beta parents (e.g. a friend trying the app with his son) can use Plan / Run /
Insights without understanding *why* the product exists: calm pacing, guided
stretching, parent-led ritual — not a meal planner. A short dismissible welcome
should explain the lay of the land once so they know what to expect.

## Non-goals (sketch)

- Full interactive multi-step product tour (`product-tour` parked)
- Video / long essay onboarding
- Blocking access until the welcome is finished (dismissible)

## Notes

- Cover: why built, Plan → Run → History/Insights, familiarity ladder + pacing /
  suggestions as guidance not orders.
- Show once per household (or per parent) after signup / first login.
- Ranked after ritual polish, before beta hosting.
